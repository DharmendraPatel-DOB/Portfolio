Questions clients ask

Quick answers so you know what working with me looks like.

What do I need to send you?

Your raw footage, a reference video or a short brief. If you have a logo, brand colors, a script or caption text, send those too. Tell me the platform and the deadline.

Which formats do you deliver?

Platform-ready exports for Instagram Reels, YouTube, ads and other digital content, in the shape the platform needs (vertical 9:16, wide 16:9 or square 1:1).

How fast do you work?

My Reel workflow is around 3 reels a day. Longer videos and heavy motion work take more time, so tell me your deadline and I'll tell you what is possible.

Do you use AI in editing?

Yes, where it genuinely helps: background removal, masking, smart cuts and color assistance. The creative decisions stay with me, so the result still feels human.

Which languages do you work in?

Hindi and English. I'm comfortable with content and captions in both.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. Final review and revisions are part of my workflow before the final export, so the video ends up the way you imagined it.
